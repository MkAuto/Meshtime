Expects name, feed (the https URL), kind (the line under the name, may be empty), badge, badgeClass, and the menu's links: google, apple, outlook (feedCard in views.js). Add to calendar is a details element, like the language menu: it opens with no JS, and app.js folds it again on a click outside or Esc. Google and Outlook open their subscribe page in a new tab. Apple Calendar, and Copy link last (for any other app, and a work Outlook), copy the feed's address: each is a real link to the feed, which app.js turns into a copy button (data-copy). Without JS, or where the clipboard is refused (plain http), right-click → Copy link. data-copied / data-copy-failed: what app.js shows on it for a moment after a click. The icons are Material Icons "calendar_today" with a plus, "content_copy" and "link" (Apache License 2.0). }}
